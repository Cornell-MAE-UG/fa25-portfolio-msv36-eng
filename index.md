## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/NewPhot.png" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

I’m Madeleine Vercher, a senior at Cornell University studying Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Business for Engineers. I’ll graduate in May 2027 and continue at Cornell to earn my Master of Engineering in Engineering Management (graduating December 2027). I’m interested in how ideas become useful products: understanding what people need, exploring technical possibilities, and coordinating the work that brings a solution to life.

This past summer, I worked as a Quality and Manufacturing Engineering Intern at Jabil. My projects ranged from designing and prototyping manufacturing aids in Creo to analyzing production workflows and researching automated guided vehicle implementation. I also worked extensively with Windchill to update drawings and engineering change documentation. Working alongside engineers and operators taught me to consider how a design will be made, used, and maintained—and to ask questions before deciding what needs to change.

At Cornell, I’ve developed my technical skills through projects in robotics, mechanical design, system dynamics, and experimental analysis. I’ve helped build an autonomous robot, modeled a satellite attitude-control system, and used laboratory data to evaluate aerodynamic performance. My design coursework has also introduced me to user research and product discovery, strengthening my interest in connecting engineering decisions with the people who will use the result.

I enjoy the organizational and collaborative side of engineering as much as the technical work. As a teaching assistant for Introduction to Mechanical Design, I help students work through CAD, prototyping, and design problems. As president of Cornell’s Big Red Bears mascot organization, I coordinate volunteers, training, and event logistics. Both roles have taught me to communicate clearly, adapt when plans change, and help a team move toward a shared goal.

I’m pursuing opportunities in product development, project management, and operations where I can combine technical problem solving with creativity and leadership. I have a particular interest in themed entertainment, shaped in part by my experience as a ride operator at Busch Gardens Tampa and love for theme parks as a Floridian. Across industries, I’m drawn to work that turns thoughtful design and careful execution into something people can use and enjoy.
